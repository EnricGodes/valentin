In the year it marks **45 years of history**, Valentin Motors, a reference in the servicing, restoration and personalisation of Porsche in Spain, takes a further step with the opening of a **new workshop in Barcelona**, on **Carrer de Varsòvia, 65**.

The new premises are a natural step in the story of Valentin Motors, which since 1979 has stood for a serious interest in Porsche, combining craft and technical precision at the highest level.

## A new stage, with more space and more services

The opening of the Varsòvia 65 workshop answers not only the wish for more accessible, more modern premises, but also the need to increase operating capacity.
 With **more than 1,300 m² over two floors**, the new centre **doubles the space** of the previous workshop and makes room for key new services:

- **Bodywork and paint**
- **Electromechanics**

Its location, just **two minutes from the Ronda del Mig**, also makes it far easier to reach from anywhere in the city.

## Reorganisation in Barcelona

With this opening, the long-standing workshop on **Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes**, for decades the operating heart of the company in Barcelona, **will no longer receive customers**. All activity moves to the new site on Varsòvia, 65, where technical work and customer service are brought together.

## Classics and modern cars alike

The new workshop keeps what Valentin Motors has always been: specialist attention to **classic cars** and to **current Porsche models**, with the technology and the knowledge to do the work properly.

## Madrid

This growth in Barcelona adds to the established activity in **Madrid**, where Valentin Motors continues to run a fully operational workshop serving customers in the centre and south of the peninsula.
