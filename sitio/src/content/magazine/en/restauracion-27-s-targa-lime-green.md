## The initial state

Yes, that's right.. we are not wrong even if you see this brown metallic, the original color of this unit when it was first registered in Barcelona was the beautiful N8 Lime green.

We started this restoration of a 2.7 S targa national and single owner from the first day to date.

The order for the property is for restoration of body, mechanics and interiors.

![Appearance of the unit being disassembled](/img/magazine/23f1922a-698a5e96-b02a-4c92-bfed-3aec1eab5c68.jpg)

*Appearance of the unit in the process of disassembly*

### Jordi's opinion

Returning these units to their initial state are always motivating challenges.

The unique ownership of this 2.7 can be seen in every corner of the vehicle.

The first phase of disassembly is important to verify the status of all components and plan the restoration.
