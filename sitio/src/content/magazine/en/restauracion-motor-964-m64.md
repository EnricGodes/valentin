## M64

Throughout the 964 generation Porsche used the 3.600 cc that has provided so much reliability and satisfaction to its owners.

Boxer layout engine with 6 cylinders with 2 valves per head and double ignition.

The Mezger block is the standard of design and reliability both used on the street and in competition.

## 964.2

Some internal and external changes underwent the 964 engine generation.

The first phase of disassembly is always important to verify the condition of all the components and be able to diagnose any hidden problems.

## Engine Rehabilitation

It is worth distinguishing two different concepts:

-Engine rebuild is when we carry out complete disassembly of all engine components, screw by screw and piece by piece for subsequent measurement, cleaning and weighing and assembly to obtain an engine with maximum compression and km 0.

-Major overhaul: here at Valentin Motors we call major overhaul the intervention process of engine and gearbox decoupling and complete systems review protocol but without disassembling the complete engine.
