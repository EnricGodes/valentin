## The initial state

This 1979 SC attracted us for its body Targa and especially for its beautiful original Oak Green color.

It maintained a never-restored status, original patina and all engine components needed urgent work.

We decided to carry out a restoration in all sections of the vehicle.

![Appearance of the unit just outside the paint booth](/img/magazine/3ba88f8b-efa871d3-06cd-4c03-ad1e-94aa3ed556e6.jpg)

*Appearance of the unit just outside the paint booth*

![At the wheel of a Porsche on the motorway, late in the afternoon](/img/magazine/49e62d8d-IMG_5792.JPG)

### Jordi's opinion

Returning these units to their initial state are always motivating challenges.

You can discern what the life of the vehicle has been like, even if there have been periods of little servicing or partial work.

The first phase of disassembly is important to verify the status of all components and plan the restoration.
