Singer changed the rules of the game

A backdating is to join the pure line of the 911 between 1965 and 1973 with a more portable 911, a 964.

## Why the base 964?

The 964 is the perfect platform because it combines the classic 911 lines along with more modern features and equipment.

## Engine?

We have several options regarding the original 3.6 of 250 hp, raising it to 3.8 with 300 hp or attaching to the 3.6 a volumetric supercharger that develops 350 hp.

## Complete design by the client.

Throughout this process we guide the client in choosing interiors, carpets, upholstery, leather and, above all, custom exterior color.

We adapt the wheels that you like and suit the most along with the design of the door panels, seats and parts of the dashboard.
