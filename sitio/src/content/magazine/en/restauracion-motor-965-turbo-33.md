## 964 Turbo a legend

In the model years 1991-92, the 911 Turbo was powered by a 3,3 liter and 320 CV supercharged engine. In the model year 1993 it was equipped with a 3,6 liter engine with a power of 360 CV. Among other elements, this version was recognized by its red brake calipers.

## The initial state

This project came to us through a client from the south of Spain who had been wanting to renew the mechanical condition of his excellent unit for some time.

The engine oil dripping onto the ground and the lack of finesse and power of the engine encouraged him to contact us and get to work.

![Restoration complete engine 965 (964 turbo 3.3), 2](/img/magazine/ff41b840-foto-jordi.jpg)

### Jordi's opinion

If there is a version of the 911 generation that does not leave you indifferent, it is the 964 turbo.

Really and honestly it is an untamed machine created in Zuffenhausen.

When the huge turbo has the right speed around 2.800 rpm you have to be very clear about what you are doing and where you are so as not to get into serious trouble.

Few machines emit a feeling of fear like this one.

The first phase is always testing, testing the unit in its current state and seeing the points that really need in-depth work or that have stopped being operational over time.

We then proceed to uncouple the engine/gearbox capsule and take it to the engine room.

The rest of the vehicle went to the restoration to carefully begin to be disassembled, keeping all the hardware well and with maximum order and neatness.

Meanwhile, in the engine room work began on complete engine disassembly, complete overhaul of gearbox and transmission and clutch kit.

The assembly of the engine was carried out under the highest standards of quality and originality.

## Engine Rehabilitation

It is worth distinguishing two different concepts:

-Engine rebuild is when we carry out complete disassembly of all engine components, screw by screw and piece by piece for subsequent measurement, cleaning and weighing and assembly to obtain an engine with maximum compression and km 0.

-Major overhaul: here at Valentin Motors we call major overhaul the intervention process of engine and gearbox decoupling and complete systems review protocol but without disassembling the complete engine.

### Jordi's technical tip

The beauty of engines with a single turbo is the **LAG** that you have to control and feel right at the moment and in the situation you are in.

There are options to *“minimize”* this **lag** such as a special discharge spring or the pressure valve, but there we are already adulterating the essence of this engine.
