## Classic interiors

The work on the interiors of the classic 911 is complete in terms of research and quality.

The choice of materials and their placement is key and affects the value of the unit to a high degree.

![At the wheel of a Porsche on the motorway, late in the afternoon](/img/magazine/49e62d8d-IMG_5792.JPG)

### Jordi's opinion

One of the most delicate moments in restoration are the interiors. Choosing the fabrics, quality and work well is a key process in any restoration.
