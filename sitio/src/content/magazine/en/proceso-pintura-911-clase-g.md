*911 G*

Standard process of body and painting in classic 911

## Main reasons?

Restorations or complete paint refresh on classic 911 that already require these works.

## When to do it?

It is possibly one of the most complex decisions to make when undertaking external paint refreshment on classic 911.

Complete vehicle disassembly work for proper preparation of the body and anchoring.
