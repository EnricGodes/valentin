## 911 Turbo generation 964, the last rear-wheel drive

The last version with a single turbocharger and rear-wheel drive was the 964 generation.

Subsequently, the 993 was equipped with two turbos and 4 wheel drive, which provided its owners with a more digestible drive compared to the brutality of the power delivery of the 964 and its rear-wheel drive.

## The initial state

This unit was acquired in France in a normal state of conservation but with a multitude of pending works on body, interiors and mechanics.

In the mechanical section we had air intakes through the intake, hot start problems and massive oil leaks from engine block.

The order was global and the objective was clear, to return the unit to its maximum splendor but with a special request from the client: to have 400 hp instead of the 320 hp.

![](/img/magazine/e5b5db46-IMG_5792.JPG)

### Jordi's opinion

*Oldschool* is the word that summarizes the entire essence of the 964 turbo.

That still high sitting position with that steering wheel “far away” and that feeling that it doesn't go up in revolutions until there is something that supernaturally pushes you forward and everything accumulates at once.

Yes, you have to be very attentive and very focused to handle a 964 turbo well. Knowing where we have the weights just at the moment when the power is very high in a very short rpm range is the key.

Sweat, you sweat a lot. Pure *motorsport*.

The first phase is always testing, testing the unit in its current state and seeing the points that really need in-depth work or that have stopped being operational over time.

Subsequently we proceed to uncouple the engine/gearbox capsule and take it to the engine room.

This part of the vehicle went to the restoration to carefully begin to be disassembled, keeping all the hardware well and with maximum order and neatness.

The body was left completely naked and the sheet metal and paint sanitation processes began.

Meanwhile, in the engine room work began on complete engine disassembly, complete overhaul of gearbox and transmission and clutch kit.

The assembly of the engine was carried out under the highest standards of quality and originality.

## Engine Rehabilitation

It is worth distinguishing two different concepts:

-Engine rebuild is when we carry out complete disassembly of all engine components, screw by screw and piece by piece for subsequent measurement, cleaning and weighing and assembly to obtain an engine with maximum compression and km 0.

-Major overhaul: here at Valentin Motors we call major overhaul the intervention process of engine and gearbox decoupling and complete systems review protocol but without disassembling the complete engine.

### The opinion of Jordi's sports preparation

The mechanical preparation of this mechanic to reach 400 hp was mainly based on:

- Special valve springs.
- Special unloading spring
- Pressure sensor
- Special camshafts.
- Turbo custom made in Germany.
- Complete exhaust system
