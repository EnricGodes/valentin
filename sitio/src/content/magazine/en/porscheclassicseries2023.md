## Castellolí 22/04/23

### First round of the season and first victory.

On Saturday 22 in April, the first competition of the 2023 season of the Porsche Classic Series was held at the Castellolí speed circuit.

Valentin Motors displayed its first event 2023 in the MotorSport section with 3 units in the Box.

August Navarro with 981 GTS **victory** in GT400 in the GT LAP SERIES

Jordi Edo with 991 GT3 Cup **first absolute place**.

### Circuit assistance

Of the three units on the track 964 RS, 981 GTS and 991 cup, two of them won the absolute victory in this first round of the season
