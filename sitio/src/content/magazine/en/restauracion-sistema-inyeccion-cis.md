Complete restoration complete CIS system

## Main reasons?

When we rebuild a complete engine, it is not just the mechanical parts, the injection plays a main role in the entire process and in the final operation of the job.

**WE PERFORM SYSTEM RESTORATIONS FOR OTHER WORKSHOPS**

## Components

Work on the probe plate, check the cold injector, clean up and rebuild the distributor, replace membranes, gaskets, clean up the internal cylinder, rebuild the regulator, regulator resistance, filter, throttle before starting, auxiliary air valve, and bench check.
