## The initial state

This 930 arrived at our facilities after having suffered a fire in the engine area, probably due to the age of the gasoline pipes or *fittings* that caused the fire that was not possible to put out until after forty minutes.

Time is key in fires and this time it completely destroyed 75% of the engine components as you can see in the disassembly photos.

![Restoration 930 incencio](/img/magazine/0aa6bda0-f19.jpg)

*Appearance of the unit that recently arrived at our workshops on Gran Via in Barcelona in July 2022*

![At the wheel of a Porsche on the motorway, late in the afternoon](/img/magazine/49e62d8d-IMG_5792.JPG)

### Jordi's opinion

We love challenges of this type.

Having the opportunity and the client's trust placed in our work pushes us to carry out these projects that are undoubtedly valued for the details.

One of the phases was to find out what upholstery it had from the factory, since in a previous restoration the entire interior was upholstered in black, then we discovered that its interior was in the beautiful *copper* combined with black and sky white that we will recover.

The first phase of disassembly is important to verify the status of all components and plan the restoration.
