*Departure to Catellolí*

A few years ago we started meeting sporadically to go on weekend outings with some clients. Little by little the interest in these outings grew and today it has become an excuse to **bring together people with common concerns** around an activity.

Participation **is open to everyone** and is **free**, with the only expense being those derived from the activity: restaurants, circuit rental, communications system rental, etc.

## Our activities

Every two months, if socio-sanitary circumstances allow, we hold an event, alternating cultural outings with driving courses or stretches of road. **These are not competitive activities**, we look for moments of socialization with Porsche owners with whom we share affinities. We have become a group of friends who organize a getaway from time to time.

> "The Club is a good way to share time with people who have the same tastes, the same passion. There is always gastronomy involved, my other passion. I really like when we do historic rally stages and how you prepare the roadbooks"

> — Carlos A.

### Track days

We usually rent a circuit a few kilometers from Barcelona where we do runs, when we can enjoy sporty driving of our vehicles. We have visited almost all the runways in Catalonia, we have even driven inside an airport.

### Driving courses

We carry out sports driving courses, safe driving courses.. moments where, guided by professionals, we acquire new driving skills.

### Endurance

The endurance format consists of a one or two-day outing where we travel through legendary stretches of public roads that we combine with gastronomic events.

### Cultural activities

Taking advantage of the fact that we are a group of people with common interests, we seek to combine activities that do not focus exclusively on driving: visits to wineries, wine tastings, views of private collections,…

### Outlaws

An outing format in a small group, usually during the week with a certain dose of improvisation.

## How to sign up

The only essential requirement is to **possess a Porsche vehicle in perfect driving condition**. The activities are not restricted to any specific model, anyone can participate: classics, youngtimers, contemporary.
We will announce the activities in advance through our [social networks](https://www.instagram.com/valentinmotors) or through [our newsletter](/contacto).

### 9/11 Rebel Ride

Date: November 2019
Location: Inland Catalonia

On Saturday 11 November, from Valentin Motors we wanted to pay tribute to this important date for us, as Porsche lovers that we are. A very special day, since on this occasion **11/9** fell on a Saturday and, obviously, we could not miss this opportunity. So we gathered some other **"911** enthusiasts to join us for this unique event, the **"9/11 Rebel Ride"**.

Neither more nor less than **20 magnificent** vehicles followed us along the roads of inland Catalonia, on a route that we divided into 5 spectacular sections: Pinos, Hostalnou, Vilaredes, Taurons and La Bassa. We enjoyed driving, at a very good pace, on beautiful and technical roads, suitable for all drivers. A morning in which we encountered fog and clouds, but in the end the sun smiled on us for a good part of the day, which we ended with a meal at the renowned restaurant **Aligué**, a delight for our palates. The perfect ending to such a memorable day, because it was our day.

### The Grand Tour Tribute by VM

Date: May 2019
Location: La Seu d’Urgell airport

This time we decided to give our mythical road routes a twist and do something different in a very special terrain, an **airport**, specifically that of La Seu d'Urgell. And why not use an airstrip to create a "**track day"** for our Porsche? And so we did it. On Saturday 4 of May our "The Grand Tour Tribute" took place, during a morning full of fun and anecdotes that we experienced together with our participants, some great riders.

A splendid day of sunshine accompanied us the entire day, which began punctually at 10 in the morning. After the usual briefing, the 3 groups into which the attendees were divided, each went to their area. The airport was divided **into 3 spectacular circuits**, one of them more demanding, with a longer route and accompanied by a curve passed through water, the other stood out for its fun slalom, and finally the last and the most different, where our participants had to put aside the Porsche to get into a Beetle that we converted into a rear-wheel drive, having to do an exercise to learn how to do countersteering, since the rear axle had the idler wheels, quite a challenge. Once the morning is over, we head to eat and rest at the splendid Aravell Golf & Country Club, where we end the day.

### Trackday Sensations: Castellolí circuit

Date: June 2017
Location: Fast Parcmotor Castellolí

Last Saturday 17 June Valentin Motors organized a **Trackday Sensations** where the clients of the **workshop specialist Porsche in Barcelona** were able to enjoy the facilities of the [**Fast Campus Parcmotor**](http://www.fastparcmotor.com/). Following the instructions of the David Bosch team, they performed maneuvers in complete safety, knowing the vehicle's reactions and rectifying erroneous actions behind the wheel.

In welcoming the attendees, the *partners* were presented. Personnel displaced by [**Michelin**](http://www.michelin.es/neumaticos/neumaticos-turismos?gclid=CjwKCAjwsqjKBRBtEiwAkWyN34Yje8y2clbwTmjbRvXYijK_CZ_91meSRRPMZECfLqvR0Nl00fq-GBoCOx0QAvD_BwE&gclsrc=aw.ds#type=type_2?xtor=SEC-3030-GOO-[[*Adgroup*]]--S-[%2Bmichelin]&xts=461138)made an explanation of the sports range. Making known the different assembly possibilities for the brand's vehicles.

Next, a walking reconnaissance of the track guided by professional driver David Bosch of the different layouts and asphalts. Once the three groups identified by color, blue, red and yellow, were created, they were ready and eager to enter the track.

Attendees had the opportunity to test Pilot Sport Cup tires 2 on rims 17 and 18 and Pilot Sport 2 on rims 18 with Porsche (N) markings. The three games provided by Michelin. Their change was carried out in the pits. Two tents supplied by [**KW**](http://www.iccpremiumstyling.com/kw_suspensions/index.html) where three mechanics from Valentin Motors carried out changes and pressure checks.

In addition, two vehicles with the suspension KW V1 and V3 so that customers could test them on the track and be able to see first-hand the behavior of these suspensions.

Once again [**Mobil1**](https://mobiloil.com/en) was present. On this occasion, it is essential to carry out levels for those vehicles that could not go through workshop before going to the circuit. You should not overlook checking levels and pressures to enter the track.

Valentin Motors presented for the first time the latest **reconstruction work on a classic Porsche** carried out by the team. Assembly of KW suspensions and replacement of front and rear ball joints. Complete engine rebuild and replacement of the exhaust set. Regarding the paint, front and rear bumpers, *fuchs* rims in matte and glossy black. Labeling and vinyl to suit the client.

The Porsche 3.2 unit from the year 72 was driven by David and Jordi to corroborate the excellent work of the Valentin Motors technical team. Taking advantage of the occasion, a report was made that will soon appear in one of the editions of the only magazine in Spanish specialized in Porsche, [**9ONCE Plus.**](http://9onceplus.com/)

### Summer Ride 2017: Next Level

Date: June 2017
Location: Fast Parcmotor of Castellolí

We go to the next level. On **Saturday 17 June** we prepared together with the **David Bosch** team from the [**Fast Parcmotor de Castellolí**](http://www.fastparcmotor.com/) an advanced course to **learn to control the vehicle** in more complex risk situations. How to carry out preventive driving to avoid loss of vehicle control and anticipate driving risks.

It was an ideal opportunity for all those riders who aim to increase their driving safety and learn about the behavior of their unit. The participation of riders with different units gives us the possibility of observing the very different reactions that Porsche brand vehicles have. It's incredible how a 964 vs 997 draws a curve on the school circuit.

Sometimes on the road we reach high-risk situations and, with ignorance of the vehicle's behavior, erroneous actions are carried out. On the track and with **personalized advice** from the instructors, maneuvers can be performed in complete safety. Since the track is equipped for it.

Riders will receive instructions and corrections from instructors via walkie-talkie.
