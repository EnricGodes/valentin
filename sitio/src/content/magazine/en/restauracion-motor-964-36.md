## M6401

Throughout the 964 generation Porsche used the 3.600 cc that has provided so much reliability and satisfaction to its owners.

Boxer layout engine with 6 cylinders with 2 valves per head and double ignition.

The Mezger block is the standard of design and reliability both used on the street and in competition.

## The initial state

Here we have a common example of massive oil leaks from engine block which forces us to open the entire engine.

![At the wheel of a Porsche on the motorway, late in the afternoon](/img/magazine/49e62d8d-IMG_5792.JPG)

### Jordi's opinion

If our 964 is already leaking oil through the block, we have no choice but to complete rehabilitation.

This requires complete disassembly of the engine and replacement of internal wear parts such as piston rings, connecting rod bearings, main bearings, pump half-bearings, chains and a complete set of upper and lower gaskets.

We recommend replacing the 24 block studs with the “dilavar” ones to avoid an unexpected breakage of one of them.

Nicasil cylinders give us an almost unlimited life.

In our case, the process of cleaning, decarbonization, verification and weighing of pieces is key.

The first phase of disassembly is always important to verify the condition of all the components and be able to diagnose any hidden problems.

## Engine Rehabilitation

It is worth distinguishing two different concepts:

-Engine rebuild is when we carry out complete disassembly of all engine components, screw by screw and piece by piece for subsequent measurement, cleaning and weighing and assembly to obtain an engine with maximum compression and km 0.

-Major overhaul: here at Valentin Motors we call major overhaul the intervention process of engine and gearbox decoupling and complete systems review protocol but without disassembling the complete engine.

### Jordi's technical tip

We recommend always replacing the delco belt that distributes the two ignitions.

To date it is not supplied originally but can be easily found aftermarket.

Always make valve guides and seats and especially nock lines.
