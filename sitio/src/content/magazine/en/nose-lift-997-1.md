*electric front axle lifting system*

For vehicles with coilover suspension or low springs

If we want to preserve the front lips or simply not damage the lower parts of the front bumper, this system using an internal button raises the front axle in 2 seconds.

## What do we gain?

Above all, peace of mind if we are one of those who are constantly suffering from causing some damage to our Porsche since we increased the ground clearance by 100 mm.

## Why do it?

In the s991 and 992 variant the manufacturer offers it as a factory option and above all, highly recommended in the GT, S

## Does it affect the value of the unit?

We are convinced that it is an upgrade that does not affect the originality and does affect the comfort of the system.
