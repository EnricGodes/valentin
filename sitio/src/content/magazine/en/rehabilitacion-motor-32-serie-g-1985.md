*engine block already closed*

Complete process of engine rebuild of a 3.2 G series.

## Main reasons?

Mainly due to non-optimal engine compression testing and oil leaks from engine block.

## Tests?

We perform engine compression tests.

## Oil leaks

The most likely are the timing and lower part rocker covers, although the upper part is made together.

If the oil leaks are from the block, it forces us to carry out a complete engine repair.
